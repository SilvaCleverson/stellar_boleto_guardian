In Mainnet

Boleto Guardian

Bank-slip authenticity

The company registers each bank slip on the Stellar blockchain. The payer validates in seconds with the 44 to 48 barcode digits — no app, no sign-up, no technical jargon.

  • Public, immutable and auditable proof of every issued slip
  • Zero-friction validation: the slip is its own lookup credential
  • Native registration via Manage Data — no smart contracts
  • No personal data on-chain (LGPD compliant)

PayOrder W3 Guardian

Web3 billing rail

Turns digital charges from any source into payment orders verifiable on-chain. Before paying, the user sees who will receive, the amount and the on-chain proof.

  • The destination wallet is never typed — it is pulled from the payee's record
  • Eliminates manual errors and phishing / man-in-the-middle fraud
  • Non-custodial payment: the user's seed never touches the backend
  • Public, immutable proof that the charge is legitimate
